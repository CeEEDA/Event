<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user initially requested a portal for monitoring power generation assets (DSE generators, Messkoffer cases) with remote control capabilities and integration with their EpiRent ERP system.

The project has since expanded significantly with a new major feature: a complete **Kirmes (Fairground) Billing System**.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS (Kirmes Billing System):**
1.  **Event Management:** Admins can create/manage events, defining dates, planning periods (Dispo-Zeitraum), and pricing.
2.  **Price Management:** Admins can set a standard price list and override it per event. The pricing model includes:
    *   Per-connection-type fees (Schuko, 16A, 32A, etc.).
    *   A general kWh price.
    *   A general handling surcharge.
    *   An estimated average kWh consumption per connection type to calculate a security deposit.
3.  **Public Exhibitor Portal:** A public-facing page where exhibitors (Schausteller) can:
    *   Register a new account (with email verification).
    *   Log in with an existing account.
    *   Select a published event and sign up by providing their ride name (Fahrgeschäft), plot number, and required power connection.
4.  **Exhibitor Management:** Admins can view and manage all registered exhibitors, edit their details, and enable a Purchase on Account () option, allowing them to book without an upfront payment method. Admins can also delete exhibitor accounts.
5.  **Exhibitor Invitations:** Admins can invite registered exhibitors to an event via email, which contains a direct link to the signup page.
6.  **Event Detail View:** An admin-facing page showing all signups for an event. It must include:
    *   Expandable rows to show full exhibitor contact and signup details.
    *   Columns for kWh Einbau (meter reading at installation) and kWh Ausbau (meter reading at removal), with an editable field for staff to input these values.
    *   A PDF export for the assembly list ().
    *   Placeholders for EMU meter data integration (live consumption, outages).
    *   A button to initiate the final billing process (Abrechnung).
7.  **Exhibitor History:** A new detail page for each exhibitor showing their contact info, a history of events they've attended, and a placeholder for their billing history.
8.  **Payment & Invoicing (Future):**
    *   Integrate Stripe for credit card payments to reserve a security deposit.
    *   Automate the final billing process (Abrechnung), which calculates final costs based on meter readings, generates ZUGFeRD-compliant e-invoices, and emails them to the exhibitor and the accounting department.
