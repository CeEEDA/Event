The user is building a comprehensive **Kirmes (Fairground) Billing System**.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS (Kirmes Billing System & Kirmeskiste):**
1.  **Event & Price Management:** Admins can create events, define planning periods, and set custom prices.
2.  **Public Exhibitor Portal:** A public portal for exhibitors to register (with email verification and a strong password policy), log in, manage their profile, view bookings, and download invoices.
3.  **Exhibitor Management:** Admins can manage exhibitors and enable a **Purchase on Account** option, which bypasses mandatory upfront payment.
4.  **Billing & Invoicing (Abrechnung):**
    *   A single button processes billing for an entire event.
    *   Generates **ZUGFeRD-compliant** e-invoices with a **GiroCode** (EPC QR Code) for easy payment.
    *   Invoices are automatically emailed (currently rate-limited).
5.  **EMU Meter Integration:** Live energy consumption data from EMU meters is linked to exhibitor signups.
6.  **Kirmeskiste Integration:**
    *   Automated setup script generation for a Raspberry Pi device with 4 connected EMU meters.
    *   A camera-based QR code system for admins to assign a physical meter to an exhibitor's booking on-site.
7.  **Payment Integration (Stripe):** Exhibitors must pay a security deposit via Stripe upon booking, unless Purchase on Account is enabled.
8.  **Document Management:** Admins can upload and manage event-specific documents (e.g., site plans) with drag-and-drop functionality.
9.  **Admin Dashboards:** A central dashboard to manage payments and track financial status.
10. **Mobile Responsiveness:** The entire portal must be optimized for use on tablets and smartphones.
